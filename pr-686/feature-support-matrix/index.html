Feature Support Matrix

Serving techniques AISimulate simulates for each inference engine. Supported means the technique is replayed within the limits on its linked page; Limited means common configurations are rejected or only an analytical estimate exists, as the row's note says. Techniques supported on their own do not automatically combine: Replay feature support lists the composition boundaries, and the AgentX section covers agentic traffic.
